# Domain Doctrine: UK Housing & Homelessness Rights

**Jurisdiction:** England only (Housing Act 1996 Part VII, Homelessness Reduction Act 2017, Equality Act 2010)  
**Core Philosophy:** The Velvet Middle Path — Respectful reasonable adjustments request up front; unshakeable statutory review clock in the rear.

## 1. Statutory Invariants
- **Reasonable Adjustments (Equality Act 2010, ss. 20, 29, 149):** Anticipatory legal duty. Disabled applicants have the right to written email communication, scheduled calls only, clear bullet points, and 7-day deadlines.
- **Homelessness in Moveable Vehicle (Housing Act 1996, s.175(2)(b)):** Van-dwellers without a secure lawful pitch are statutorily homeless. Under s.175(3), a vehicle lacking adequate sanitation or heating is unreasonable to continue to occupy.
- **Interim Duty to Accommodate (s.188(1)):** Low threshold. Triggers as soon as the Council has 'reason to believe' the applicant may be homeless, eligible, and in priority need. Independent of local connection (s.188(2)).
- **Priority Need & Vulnerability (s.189(1)(c)):** Assessed under *Hotak v Southwark LBC [2015]* and *Panayiotou v Waltham Forest LBC [2017]*: significantly more vulnerable than an ordinary person facing homelessness.
- **The Strict 21-Day Clock (s.202 Review):** Any adverse s.184 decision letter must be challenged within exactly 21 calendar days.
- **Unauthorized Encampments (CJPOA 1994 s.60C / PCSC Act 2022):** Criminalization applies only if specific statutory damage/disruption elements are met and there is an unreasonable refusal to leave.
